Collaborative robot welding and cutting solutions

Collaborative welding robots

Using Sakae Global's dedicated welding program, the operator teaches the weld path by moving the torch by hand. For repeated joints, the robot reproduces the registered path, so the operator can focus on setup and checking the finish.

Besides table and cart types, configurations with travel axes are available for large work. The robot arm is from the Rainbow Robotics RB series.

Gantry / traveling type

Usable right away, even with no robot experience

No difficult operation is needed. A single straight weld can be set up in about 30 seconds.

  1. 1

    Select weld conditions by icon

    Choose weld conditions from on-screen icons.

  2. 2

    Move to the work position

    Follow the Japanese voice guidance and move the robot by hand.

  3. 3

    Check the motion

    Check the registered motion before welding.

  4. 4

    Start welding

    Press the button to weld along the registered path.

Weld patterns to choose from

Straight, curved, spot and weaving. Register them with simple teaching: just move the collaborative robot by hand.

Save and recall jobs

Programs can be saved externally to USB, a server or a PC. For repeat work, just recall them.

Options

Positioner

Rotates and tilts the workpiece to hold it in an easy welding position.

Tool changer (manual / automatic)

Change tools quickly and easily without screws. Lightweight and suited to long operation.

OLP (offline programming)

OCTOPUS software. Verifies the process by simulation and automatically checks for collisions and malfunctions.

3D vision sensor

By Photoneo. 3D scanning with point clouds for high accuracy and a wide recognition range.

Automatic seam-tracking sensor

SCANSONIC TH6D. Detects the weld seam with a laser sensor and works on highly reflective materials.

Plasma and laser applications

The gantry / traveling type can also be combined with plasma cutting or laser welding.

The robot alone or combined with a CO₂ welder is also available. For selection, tell us the workpiece size, joint shape and your existing jigs and power supply.
