How to choose a model

Laser light delivered by optical fiber is focused by the cutting head to melt the metal locally. Assist gas from the nozzle blows out the molten metal, and NC control follows the contour of the drawing. Sheet machines cut on a flat bed; pipe machines hold and rotate the tube in a chuck.

Key specifications

Example sheet working sizes
KUBE1212: 1,240 × 1,240 mm. FL3015: 3,048 × 1,524 mm. The FO series handles long plate of nominal 12, 20 and 30 m.
Pipe diameter, length and weight

The FL3015 has a maximum cutting speed of 55 m/min and combined rapid traverse of 140 m/min. Actual cutting speed varies with material, thickness, shape and gas conditions.

CAD/CAM

Works with the CAD/CAM you already use

HK laser cutting machines support MetaCAM, a sheet-metal CAD/CAM. You can create cutting programs while reusing unfolded part data made in other software.

Even in shops running press brakes from other makers, MetaCAM lets you connect an HK machine to the existing workflow, adding or renewing cutting capacity while bending stays as it is.

The data that can be linked depends on software versions and configuration, so please tell us about your current setup first. Company and product names are trademarks or registered trademarks of their owners.
