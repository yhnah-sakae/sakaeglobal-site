HK technology that cuts cost and improves finish

Proprietary technology that lowers daily running costs and downstream work.

Mixed-gas cutting

Nitrogen and oxygen mixed at an optimized ratio cut mild steel over 6 mm about 10% faster with less burr, reducing post-processing.

Low-pressure nitrogen cutting

A dedicated zoom head and chamber nozzle cut with nitrogen at just 7–8 bar. In the maker’s tests, nitrogen use fell by about 60%.

Honeycomb-structure frame

A high-strength frame that is both light and rigid, limiting deformation and holding accuracy at high speed.

Intuitive operating screen

A clear touch screen makes restarting after a pause and setting parameters simple, with online updates.

Fiber laser energy savings

Compared with a 4 kW CO2 laser, an 8 kW fiber laser uses about 22% less power and cuts thin sheet up to about five times faster (maker’s comparison).

Automation you can add in stages

From Smart Loader and Smart Cell to Smart Tower and an eight-shelf tower: automate loading, unloading and storage as far as you need.

How to choose a model

Laser light delivered by optical fiber is focused by the cutting head to melt the metal locally. Assist gas from the nozzle blows out the molten metal, and NC control follows the contour of the drawing. Sheet machines cut on a flat bed; pipe machines hold and rotate the tube in a chuck.

Key specifications

Example sheet working sizes
KUBE1212: 1,240 × 1,240 mm. FL3015: 3,048 × 1,524 mm. The FO series handles long plate of nominal 12, 20 and 30 m.
Pipe diameter, length and weight
TL6527-S: round tube Ø12–273 mm, standard length 6.5 m, up to 200 kg per piece. TL9036-X: Ø40–360 mm, standard length 9 m, up to 1,200 kg per piece.
Material, power and thickness
Cutting thickness differs for mild steel, stainless steel and aluminum. For the 6 kW RS3015, the manufacturer's values are up to 25, 20 and 15 mm respectively.
Cutting speed and rapid traverse
The FL3015 has a maximum cutting speed of 55 m/min and combined rapid traverse of 140 m/min. Actual cutting speed varies with material, thickness, shape and gas conditions.

CAD/CAM

Works with the processes and software you already use

HK laser cutting machines support MetaCAM, a sheet-metal CAD/CAM that shares processing data across machine makers and supports lasers, punches and press brakes from many brands.

Even in shops running Amada press brakes with AP100, MetaCAM lets you reuse your unfolded part data and bring an HK machine into the existing workflow.

The data that can be linked depends on software versions and configuration, so please tell us about your current setup first. Company and product names are trademarks or registered trademarks of their owners.

What MetaCAM makes possible

  • Convert and reuse part data created in other CAD/CAM software
  • Manage data for machines from several makers in one place
  • Unfolding and nesting planned with the bending step in mind
