Business

Centered on equipment sales for manufacturing sites, we run five businesses: trade in materials, parts and food; inspection and sorting; KD packing; and outsourcing support for development work.

01

Manufacturing and production-support equipment

We support the selection and installation of laser cutting machines, welders and collaborative robots. We also handle equipment for factory compressed air and shock-absorbing wheel stops for logistics and parking facilities.

02

Trade in materials, parts and food

We handle Japanese materials and parts, motors, motor cores, batteries and air-conditioning products. In food, we also work on overseas sales of Japanese confectionery and beverages.

03

Inspection and sorting services

We inspect and sort products and parts. Tell us the items, quantities, and the defects or criteria you want checked.

04

KD packing

We handle knock-down (KD) packing, shipping products as parts or units. We can advise on shipping form and packaging based on product dimensions, quantity and destination.

05

Outsourced development support

We support engineering outsourcing for development projects in Japan and abroad. Tell us the expertise, process scope and schedule you need.

Careers

Work that supports Japanese manufacturing.

You are welcome to ask about the work and the workplace in a casual meeting.

Careers and casual meeting inquiriesJob openings ›
The job
You visit manufacturing sites and propose the installation or replacement of welders, processing machines and collaborative robots. After delivery you stay with the customer and arrange inspection, repair and maintenance.
Who we are looking for
We welcome people who listen to customers, cooperate with those around them, and think and act for themselves. In selection we also ask what you want to learn and try next.
Workplace and training
Senior staff teach the work to those new to the industry. There are chances to try new ways of proposing and to work with overseas markets.
Benefits
Uniforms provided, free drinks and snacks, birthday gifts and more.
