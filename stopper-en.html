Patented

Shock-absorbing car stopper (wheel stop)

A next-generation wheel stop that absorbs and softens the impact when a tire meets it. Anyone can install it simply by setting anchors.

Shock-absorbing design

Absorbs and softens parking impact, protecting both vehicle and stopper and greatly reducing failures.

Durable, long life

No failure in a 500,000-cycle operation test. Durable materials and weather-resistant construction extend replacement intervals.

Helps restrain overrunning

Helps restrain the vehicle from climbing over if the accelerator is pressed by mistake while the drive wheels are against the stopper.

Comfortable parking

A smooth feel that makes parking reassuring for expectant mothers, people with disabilities, older people and children.

Economical

Prevents damage, greatly reducing repair costs and keeping running costs low.

Versatile

For indoor and outdoor use, various surfaces, vehicle types and bays, from construction sites to ordinary car parks and EV bays.

Note on restraining overrunning

This product does not completely prevent a vehicle from climbing over. A vehicle may climb over in cases such as the following.

  • Entering above a certain speed
  • Accelerating from a distance (when the drive wheels are not against the stopper)
  • Four-wheel-drive or high-torque vehicles

Recommended locations

  • Key facilities and construction sites (protecting costly vehicles and equipment)
  • Hazardous areas and areas with a risk of falls (a safer working environment)
  • Ordinary car parks (preventing vehicle damage, longer life)

Solving problems with conventional stops

  • Broken blocks → shock-absorbing design prevents breakage
  • Damage to vehicles → two-stage absorption halves the impact
  • Blocks coming loose → firm anchoring design

Colors, orders and installation

  • Colors: yellow / blue / green / pink / red (except stainless steel)
  • Made to order: ask us about trucks, buses and large vehicles
  • Easy installation: anchor-bolt fixing, quick to fit
  • LED models stay visible in dark places
