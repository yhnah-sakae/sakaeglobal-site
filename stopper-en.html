How it differs from ordinary stoppers

From stoppers that break on impact to one that absorbs it

Absorbs the impact, limiting damage

The body takes up the force of the tyre, so the stopper itself is hard to break.

Two-stage absorption softens the shock

A spring and the rotating bar reduce the shock passed to the vehicle.

Fixed firmly with anchor bolts

Anchoring to the surface makes installation quick and keeps the stopper in place.

How it works

Shock absorbed in two stages

STEP 1

The stop bar takes the load

When the tyre touches, the bar moves back and a spring absorbs the shock.

STEP 2

The rotating bar lets the force escape

The outer part of the bar rotates to ease the remaining force, protecting both vehicle and stopper.

Features

Six features

Shock-absorbing design

Absorbs and softens parking impact, protecting both vehicle and stopper and greatly reducing failures.

Durable, long life

No failure in a 500,000-cycle operation test. Durable materials and weather-resistant construction extend replacement intervals.

Note on restraining overrunning

This product does not completely prevent a vehicle from climbing over. A vehicle may climb over in cases such as the following.

  • Entering above a certain speed
  • Accelerating from a distance (when the drive wheels are not against the stopper)
  • Four-wheel-drive or high-torque vehicles
